---
summary: "Site for Eduardo Boigues's 2024 mayoral re-election campaign in Itaquaquecetuba (SP), with an ideas form sent to an API. React.js, Vite and Tailwind CSS."
---

A web page that introduces the candidate for mayor of Itaquaquecetuba, SP, running for re-election in 2024, and invites voters to suggest ideas for the next term.

Built with **React.js** and **Vite**, styled with **Tailwind CSS**.

The form is easy to fill out on any device and respects the more private details, which the user chooses whether or not to send.

The ideas go to an **API**, which stores them for later analysis.
