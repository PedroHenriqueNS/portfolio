**Linkeza** is a Brazilian link shortener with **dynamic QR Codes** and **WhatsApp links that rotate between agents**, made for local businesses.

The same printed QR Code sends each customer to a randomly picked agent on WhatsApp, shows where each contact came from and can point somewhere new whenever the business needs it, with no reprinting.

It's a SaaS I'm building solo, end to end: product, architecture, the **Astro** front end, the **NestJS** API with **TypeORM**, queues and cache on **Redis**, authentication with **Clerk** and the edge on **Cloudflare**, plus the visual identity and the launch.

It's in pre-launch, with a waitlist open at linkeza.com.
