**Linkeza** is a Brazilian link shortener with **dynamic QR Codes** and **WhatsApp links that rotate between agents**, designed for local businesses.

The same printed QR Code sends each customer to a randomly picked agent on WhatsApp, shows where each contact came from and changes its destination whenever the business needs it, with no reprinting.

It is a SaaS that I am building alone, end to end: product, architecture, front end in **Astro**, API in **NestJS** with **TypeORM**, queues and cache with **Redis**, authentication with **Clerk** and the edge on **Cloudflare**, plus the visual identity and the launch.

It is in pre-launch, with a waitlist open at linkeza.com.
