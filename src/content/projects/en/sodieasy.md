---
summary: "Mobile app to quote Sodimac construction materials by product ID, with a Node.js API that scrapes prices from the web."
---

A mobile app to quote construction materials with products available at **Sodimac**.

The application makes requests to an API that performs *web scraping* of the Sodimac website from the product ID, saving the data and updating it as it changes on the official site.

The project is meant for a company sales assistant, who naturally looks products up by ID; that is why the whole search is based on the product ID.

The app is built with **React Native** and the **Expo** framework.
