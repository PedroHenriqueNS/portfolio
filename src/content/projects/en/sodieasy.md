---
summary: "Mobile app for building quotes for Sodimac construction materials by product ID, with a Node.js API that scrapes the prices."
---

A mobile app for putting together quotes for construction materials available at **Sodimac**.

The app calls an API that *scrapes* the Sodimac website by product ID, saving the data and updating it as it changes on the official site.

The project is meant for a staff member who already looks products up by ID; that's why every search is based on the product ID.

The app is built in **React Native** with the **Expo** framework.
