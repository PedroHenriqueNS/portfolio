---
summary: "Desktop graphical interface in Python (CustomTkinter) for yt-dlp: downloads videos from any supported site and converts quality and format."
---

A desktop app to download videos from any site that **yt_dlp** supports.

The application is built around the **yt_dlp** package, with the goal of giving it a graphical interface.

Besides downloading videos in high quality from any supported site, it can also convert quality and format.

The **CustomTkinter** package was used for the graphical interface.
