---
summary: "Desktop GUI in Python (CustomTkinter) for yt-dlp: downloads videos from any supported site and converts quality and format."
---

A desktop app for downloading videos from any site that **yt_dlp** supports.

The app is built around the **yt_dlp** package, to give it a graphical interface.

Besides downloading videos from any supported site in high quality, it can also convert quality and format.

The interface uses the **CustomTkinter** package.
